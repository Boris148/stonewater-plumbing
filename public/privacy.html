StoneWater Plumbing Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

StoneWater Plumbing ("we", "us") serves homeowners in the San Antonio, Texas area. This policy explains what we collect when you request a free water test or contact us.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, email address, home address, and answers you give about your home and water (for example, city or well water).

How we use it

Only to contact you about your request, schedule your water test, give you a quote, and follow up about our water treatment and plumbing services. We may contact you by phone call, text message, or email. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the licensed plumbing technicians who perform your service, and with service providers that help us run our business (such as our customer management software), who must keep it confidential.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, call or text (210) 781-6251.
